The **Ducati Panigale V4** is a high-performance sportbike introduced in **2018**, featuring a **1,103 cc desmodromic 90° V4 engine** derived from Ducati’s MotoGP **Desmosedici** racing engine. It replaces the **1299 V-twin** and is currently the **fastest road-legal motorcycle**, reaching **317 km/h (197 mph)**. A **998 cc version** meets **WSBK regulations**. Ducati’s **first mass-produced V4** street bike, it retains a **counter-rotating crankshaft** for better handling and uses an **aluminum perimeter frame** instead of the engine-integrated frame of previous models. The Panigale V4 incorporates **advanced electronics**, including **traction, wheelie, and drift control**, along with high-speed **cornering ABS**. Its **Brembo brakes** are lighter than previous versions, and it rides on **Pirelli Diablo Super Corsa SP tires**. Several variants enhance its performance further: the **V4 S** includes an **Öhlins electronic suspension, lightweight lithium battery, and forged aluminum wheels**; the **V4 Speciale** adds **carbon mudguards, an Alcantara seat, titanium exhaust, and a race kit** boosting power to **226 hp**; the **V4 R**, a **WSBK homologation special**, uses a **998 cc engine producing 215 hp (233 hp with race kit)** and features an **adjustable swingarm pivot, improved aerodynamics, and MotoGP-style winglets**; and the **V4 Superleggera**, limited to **500 units**, focuses on extreme weight reduction with a **carbon frame, swingarm, and wheels**, producing **221 hp (233 hp with race kit)** and achieving a **power-to-weight ratio of 1.56**, making it the **most powerful street-legal production motorcycle** ever built.
